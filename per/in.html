About Ketim

Ketim Perfumes is a small-batch perfume house based in Addis Ababa. We craft scents inspired by Ethiopian flowers, spices, and the ambience of our cities. We focus on longevity and natural-feeling blends that suit everyday wear and special moments.

  • Hand-blended in small batches
  • Locally inspired ingredients and notes
  • Face-to-face pickup or delivery inside Addis

Why choose us?

We use quality bases and carefully selected fragrance oils for long-lasting impressions. Each bottle is prepared by hand and labeled with care.
