About Our House

Founded in 2025 in Addis Ababa, Kt perfume is where luxury becomes accessible. We specialize in high-quality, hand-blended fragrances that mirror the world’s most iconic designer brands. At our boutique, we prioritize the art of the scent over the cost of the name tag, ensuring you smell premium for less.

  • Custom Scent Creation: Our specialty. We collaborate with you to mix and create a brand-new, unique fragrance designed entirely around your personal preferences.
  • Premium "Inspired-By" Scents: Affordable, high-quality recreations of global favorites like Sauvage, Baccarat, Blue de Chanel, Si, Eternity and much more.
  • Professional Refills: We refill your empty bottles with our premium blends to save you money and reduce waste.

The Handmade Difference

We avoid mass production. Each bottle is hand-poured, labeled with care, and checked for quality before it reaches your hands.
